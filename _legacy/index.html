Retries vs a circuit breaker

When a downstream service goes down, blind retries multiply the load on it and make every user sit through timeouts. A circuit breaker spots the failures, answers immediately with a fallback, then sends an occasional test request until the service recovers. I've added both to production integrations.

Try this: break the downstream, then switch between the two strategies and watch the load and wait time.
